"My name is Sam, I live with
my mom on the other side
of the river. I asked a boatman
to give me a lift to this side of
the river. I have come in
search of work. My mom is sick
and we have nothing to eat. I
want to earn some money and
give my mom food and medicines".
said the boy in a sad voice.
"Did no one give you a job
that side of the river", asked
Julian with surprise.
