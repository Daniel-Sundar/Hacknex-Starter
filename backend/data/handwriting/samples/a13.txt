13) Larynx :
         In humans, the sound is
    produced by the voice box or the
    larynx. It is at the upper end of
    the wind pipe. Two vocal cords are
    streched across the voice box in such
    a way that it leaves a narrow
    slit between them for the passage
    of air. When the lungs force air
    through the slit, the vocal cords
    vibrate, producing sound.
