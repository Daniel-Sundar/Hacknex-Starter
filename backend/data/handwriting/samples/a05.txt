Origin of the Electric Distribution Industry:
1880s
- Thomas Edison operated the Pearl Street Steam-electric plant
  and the pioneer distribution (DC) system
- 1882: water-wheel driven generator was installed in Appleton,
  Wisconsin that operated at 2400V, DC (59km travelled)
  ▷ DC had short comings because it was hard to deliver over
    long distances

- 1885: AC current by Nikola Tesla and George Westinghouse
  ▷ 4000 V AC transmission line between Oregon and Portland
- improvements and advancements led to transmission and
  generation capacity of lower + decreasing unit costs!
  ▷ but simultaneously, concentration and capacities of individual
    plants also increased investment for large projects
- we still need to improve these technologies of the our growing
  demand of electricity
  ▷ minimizing land use, water use, and environmental impact
  ▷ research is not funded
